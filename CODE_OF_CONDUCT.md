# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone participating in this project, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions, reviews, and feedback.
- Assume good intent and communicate disagreements professionally.
- Focus on technical issues and project goals.
- Accept feedback gracefully and collaborate in good faith.
- Help maintain a safe and inclusive environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Threats, intimidation, doxxing, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing private information without clear permission.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.
- Repository documentation and communication channels tied to this project.
- Public interactions where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

- Report it to the repository maintainers as soon as possible.
- Include relevant context, links, screenshots, and timestamps when available.
- If reporting publicly would be unsafe, contact a maintainer privately.

All reports will be handled as promptly and confidentially as practical.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including:

- Warning the involved party.
- Removing or editing comments, issues, discussions, or pull requests.
- Temporarily restricting participation.
- Permanently banning individuals from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions prioritize community safety and project health.

## Good-Faith Participation

We recognize that mistakes happen. When appropriate, maintainers may favor education and correction over punishment, provided participants act in good faith and stop harmful behavior.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by common open source community standards and adapted for this repository.
